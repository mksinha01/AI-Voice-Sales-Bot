Sankar Group – Intern Assignment
Build an AI Voice Sales Bot
Objective
Create a working AI voice bot that can sell any SaaS product through an interactive voice
conversation.
Requirements
1. Voice Conversation: The bot must speak with customers, understand their responses,
answer questions, explain the product, and try to convert them into customers.
2. Language: Use any language, such as English, Hindi, or Telugu.
3. Live Chat Screen: Display the AI bot's messages and the customer's responses as text
in real time.
4. Call Controls: Provide Start Call and End Call buttons, along with call duration and
status.
5. Call Recording: After the conversation ends, save the voice recording and provide an
option to play it back, with appropriate recording consent.
6. AI Summary: Automatically generate a short summary of the conversation, customer
requirements, objections, and next steps.
7. Sales Status: Automatically assign one of these statuses:
o Converted
o Interested
o Follow-up Required
o Not Interested
o Demo Requested
o No Response
8. Call History: Display previous conversations with their recordings, summaries,
transcripts, and final statuses.
Submission
• Working website link.
• Brief explanation of how it works in video
Deadline and Evaluation

• You may use any suitable tools, AI platforms, no-code tools, or programming
languages.
• Focus on functionality and user experience rather than complex development.
• A static design or prerecorded conversation alone will not be accepted.
Final Goal: Demonstrate an AI sales bot that can conduct a real voice conversation, display
live chat, record the call, generate an AI summary, and show the final sales status.
